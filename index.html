Notes: Withholding ≈ what payroll takes, not your final April tax. 401(k) amounts are still your money. Model verified to the penny against real 2026 Atlas pay stubs (federal withholding, Medicare and CA DT all reproduced exactly). CBA 3.C.3 hours: Day 1 = 2, Day 2 = 4, Day 3 = 6; beyond 3 days past day 3 it is 12/day if involuntarily extended (3.C.3.d), 6/day if voluntary or Open Time (3.C.3.e), and 6/day for Open Time flying subsequently involuntarily extended (the carve-out in 3.C.3.d). Not applicable to training days (3.C.3.f). Bid-month rollover: the day ladder is a running count of open-time/extension days within a bid month (CBA 25.D.2 — January runs the 1st to the 30th, February from Jan 31 to Mar 1, March from the 2nd to the 31st, every other month is the calendar month), so a trip crossing that boundary restarts at Day 1 (2 hrs). Set the “Open-time day #” field to where this trip picks up in the current month’s running count. Tax constants: IRS Pub 15-T (2026) percentage method, annual, MFJ, W-4 Step 2 checkbox checked; SS 6.2% to $184,500; Medicare 1.45% + 0.9% over $200,000; CA SDI/VDI 1.30% no cap; 24 pay periods/year.
